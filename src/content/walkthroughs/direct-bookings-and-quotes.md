## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Direct Bookings and Quotes help hosts convert enquiries without relying only on OTAs. You can start from the Calendar to create a direct reservation, or use Quotes to build a property or service offer for a guest. Add the customer, dates, listing, pricing, fees, and any services, then send the quote or confirm the reservation once payment is received. Once confirmed, the booking connects back into Calendar, Reservations, Messages, Operations, and reporting, so the team can manage it like any other stay."
