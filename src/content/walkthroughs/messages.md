See how Messages works as the main guest inbox: search and filter conversations, open a guest thread, switch channels for the same guest, use the drawer for bookings and quotations, translate replies, create tasks, and escalate or assign conversations to teammates.

## Recording Flow

1. Open Messages and show filters, status tabs, channel badges, and conversation count.
2. Open one guest conversation and show guest/reservation context.
3. Point to Autopilot, Concierge, Message Automation, and Scheduled Messages.
4. Explain the difference between planned messages and AI answers.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Messages is the shared guest inbox for SympleHost. It brings conversations from connected channels into one place, so your team can search, filter, reply, assign, and escalate without jumping between apps. The channel badge shows where the message came from, and the side panel gives guest, customer, property, and reservation context. For automation, there are two ideas to keep separate: Automated Messages are planned SOP messages like check-in instructions or review requests, while Concierge is the AI assistant that answers live guest questions using property data, notes, documents, rules, and past chats."
