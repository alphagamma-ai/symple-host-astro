Explain the payment foundation for direct bookings and confirmations.

## Recording Flow

1. Open Settings -> Integrations or Payments.
2. Show Stripe setup at a high level.
3. Explain partial payment schedules and deposits.
4. Connect the idea back to confirmed bookings and automated workflows.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Payments is a key setup step for hosts taking direct bookings. Connect Stripe first, then configure how guests pay: full payment, partial payment, or deposit collection depending on your rules. Partial payments let you collect part of the booking value first and the remaining balance later. Deposits help manage security or damage policies. Once payment is received, make sure the reservation is confirmed, because confirmed bookings are what your team and automated workflows should trust."
