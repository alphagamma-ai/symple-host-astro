## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Listings is where your properties live in SympleHost. Each listing holds the information guests and channels rely on: property details, units, photos, amenities, policies, pricing, availability, and channel connections. For most hosts, the easiest starting point is importing from Airbnb, then connecting other channels after the listing is inside SympleHost. If you want to start with Booking.com, use Channel Manager and connect through Channex. Once the listing is set up properly, the rest of the platform becomes much easier: calendar, reservations, guest messages, guide books, and reports all depend on clean listing data."
