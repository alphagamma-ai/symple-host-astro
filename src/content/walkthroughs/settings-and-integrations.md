Make it obvious that Settings lives under the top-right profile menu.

## Recording Flow

1. Click the top-right profile menu and open Settings.
2. Show General, company/workspace details, integrations, task reminders, and user/account areas.
3. Open Integrations and scan channels such as WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, LINE, Gmail, Stripe, and pricing tools where available.
4. End by reminding users to come here whenever they need account-level configuration.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Settings is where account and workspace configuration lives. Open it from the top-right profile menu. From here you can manage workspace details, integrations, users, task reminders, payments, messaging channels, and other account-level controls. Integrations are especially important during setup: connect the channels your business uses, such as messaging tools, Stripe, pricing tools, and supported OTA or channel connections. If something affects the whole workspace rather than one booking or one task, Settings is usually the place to look."
