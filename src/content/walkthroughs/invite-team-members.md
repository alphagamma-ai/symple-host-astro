Use this walkthrough when you want to give someone access to your SympleHost account. It shows how to open Team Members, invite a teammate, pick a role, and send the invitation.

## What You’ll Do

1. Open **Settings → Team Members**.
2. Click **Add Team Member**.
3. Enter their name, email, and phone number.
4. Choose a role that matches what they need to do.
5. Send the invitation email (or activate the account instantly).
6. Confirm they appear on the team list with the right status.

## Good To Know

Only the Account Owner and Account Admins can manage team members. After someone accepts the invite, their sidebar and permissions follow the role you assigned. See [How to Add Team Members & Assign Roles](/getting-started/add-team-members-and-roles/) for the full role guide.
