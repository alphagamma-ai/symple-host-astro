Learn how to compose a guide book, add sections and images, attach it to a listing, and send guests a shareable URL that updates whenever you edit.

## Recording Flow

1. Open Guide Books and show the list.
2. Open a guide and scan check-in, house rules, Wi-Fi, local recommendations, and images.
3. Show the share/link concept.
4. Explain why guide books reduce repeated guest questions.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Guide Books let you create a digital guest guide for each property. Add check-in instructions, Wi-Fi, parking, house rules, appliance notes, local recommendations, and images, then share the guide with guests through a link. The advantage is that your team keeps one source of truth. If something changes, update the guide once and the shared version stays current. Guide Books also help Concierge answer better, because clear property knowledge reduces repeat questions and keeps guest communication consistent."
