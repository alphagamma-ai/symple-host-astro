Use this demo to explain when to use the Reservations module instead of the Calendar.

## Recording Flow

1. Open Reservations and show the bookings list.
2. Filter or search for a guest.
3. Open a reservation and show guest details, source, dates, payment state, and status.
4. Explain why confirming bookings matters for downstream automation.

## Steps Shown

Open Reservations, open a booking, create manual reservations when needed, and confirm paid bookings.
