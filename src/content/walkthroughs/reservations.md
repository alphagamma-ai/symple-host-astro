Use this demo to explain when to use the Reservations module instead of the Calendar.

## Recording Flow

1. Open Reservations and show the bookings list.
2. Filter or search for a guest.
3. Open a reservation and show guest details, source, dates, payment state, and status.
4. Explain why confirming bookings matters for downstream automation.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Reservations is the detailed booking workspace. The Calendar is best for visual availability, but Reservations is where you check the full booking record: guest details, property, dates, source, payment status, and reservation status. This is also where teams can confirm whether a booking is ready for operational follow-up. If you have received payment, confirm the booking so the platform can treat it as active. If a booking is not confirmed, some downstream workflows, including automated guest messages, may not fire as expected."
