Use this demo for hosts who sell services alongside stays.

## Recording Flow

1. Open Services and show existing services.
2. Open a service and show type, schedule, pricing, provider/team, and booking options.
3. Mention tour types, multi-day services, calendar updates, and shift scheduling where relevant.
4. Explain how services can support upsells and guest experience.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Services is where hosts manage extras beyond accommodation: tours, transfers, experiences, add-ons, cleaning, or other bookable services. Each service can have its own type, schedule, price, availability, provider, and booking rules. For experience operators, this can include tour options and multi-day services. For property managers, it can support operational services or guest upsells. The goal is to make services bookable, trackable, and connected to the guest journey instead of handled manually through messages and spreadsheets."
