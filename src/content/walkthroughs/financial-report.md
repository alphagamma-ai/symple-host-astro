## Recording Flow

1. Open Financial Reports.
2. Filter by date range and property.
3. Show revenue, fees, payouts, channel split, and owner statements.
4. Explain when to use this for owner reporting and performance reviews.

## 1-Minute Voiceover

"Financial Reports help you understand performance across properties and channels. Filter by listing and date range to review revenue, payouts, fees, and booking source performance. For property managers, this is especially useful when preparing owner statements or explaining how a property performed over a month. The goal is to move reporting out of spreadsheets and into one place where reservations, channels, and financial summaries connect."
