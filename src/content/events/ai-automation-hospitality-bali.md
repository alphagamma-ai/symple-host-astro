## Get practical with AI before the market makes it mandatory.

Hospitality and short-term rentals are moving into a new operating era. Guests expect faster replies, cleaner handoffs, more personal service, and fewer mistakes. Teams are already stretched, and adding more dashboards does not solve the problem.

This Bali session is designed for hosts, villa operators, property managers, and hospitality teams who want a practical view of where AI and automation can help today.

Max Del Vita, CEO and Co-founder of Symplehost.ai and Claude Community Ambassador, will walk through how AI is changing the way hospitality teams manage guest communication, operations, listings, bookings, and daily decisions.

The goal is simple: help operators understand what is real, what is hype, and how to start using AI in a way that makes the business easier to run.

No. The session is designed for operators, founders, managers, and teams who want practical ways to benefit from AI without becoming technical specialists.

Yes. Max will use SympleHost examples to make the workflows concrete, especially around guest messaging, listing onboarding, and day-to-day operations.
