> 📘 **Step 4 of 5** in [Set Up Guest Messaging: The Complete Guide](/platform-guides/get-started-with-messages-ai-and-automation/)

**Autopilot decides what Concierge is allowed to do with real guests: stay quiet, draft replies for you to approve, or reply on its own. Most hosts start on Suggestion and switch to On once they trust the answers.**

---

> **TL;DR:** Go to **Messages → Settings → Current Autopilot** and pick **Off**, **Suggestion** or **On**. Start with **Suggestion**: Concierge drafts every reply and you decide what gets sent. You can also change the mode for one guest from the conversation itself.

---

## The Three Modes

| Mode | What happens | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Off** | Concierge doesn't reply. You answer every message yourself. | While you're still setting up |
| **Suggestion** | Concierge drafts a reply. You review it, edit it if needed, and send it. | Your first weeks, or if you like to check every message |
| **On** | Concierge replies by itself after a short delay. | Once its test answers are consistently right |

Think of **Suggestion** like a new team member who writes drafts for you to check before anything goes out. **On** is the same team member after they've earned your trust.

Before you choose Suggestion or On, set up what Concierge knows. See [Set Up Your Concierge](/platform-guides/organise-concierge-knowledge/).

---

## How to Choose a Mode

1. Go to **Messages** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Settings** tab at the top.
3. Click **Current Autopilot** in the left menu.
4. Click **Off**, **Suggestion** or **On**.

![The Autopilot settings page with the Off, Suggestion and On cards, the Response delay box and the Pause all automated guest messages switch](/screenshots/autopilot-off-suggestion-on/autopilot-mode.png)

### Response delay

When Autopilot is **On**, Concierge waits before it replies. The default is **60 seconds** and the minimum is **20 seconds**. The wait gives your team a chance to jump in first, and it keeps replies from feeling robotic.

---

## Working in Suggestion Mode

In Suggestion mode, Concierge writes a draft whenever a guest messages you. You'll find it in the conversation in **Messages**, in a **Suggested Reply** box.

For each draft, you can:

- **Send Now**: send it as it is.
- **Edit**: change the wording, then send.
- **Preview**: see exactly what the guest will get.
- **Dismiss**: throw the draft away and write your own reply.

If the guest sends another message before you reply, the older draft is replaced with a new one, so you only ever have one draft to review.

💡 **Tip:** If a draft is wrong, fix the source instead of just editing the draft. Add the missing fact to the listing's document or add a rule. That way the next guest gets the right answer too.

## Change the Mode for One Guest

Sometimes you want Concierge to step back for one guest, for example a VIP or a guest with a complaint, without changing it for everyone.

1. Open the conversation in **Messages**.
2. Find **Autopilot** in the bar above the messages.
3. Click it to switch between **Suggesting**, **Auto-replying** and **Off**.

![The conversation bar showing Stay, Property and Autopilot set to Suggesting](/screenshots/autopilot-off-suggestion-on/conversation-autopilot-pill.png)

This only changes that guest. Everyone else keeps your account-wide mode.

## Handing a Conversation to Your Team

Concierge passes a conversation to you when it can't answer confidently, or when a rule is set to **Avoid**. You can also do it yourself:

1. Open the conversation and click **Escalate**.
2. Add a note so your team knows why.
3. Click to confirm. The conversation moves to the **Escalated** list for the whole team.

When the issue is sorted, click **Resolve escalation**.

SympleHost also spots conversations that look finished, for example when the guest says it's sorted, has checked out, or hasn't written for 3 days. You'll see **Looks resolved** with **Confirm resolved** and **Keep open**.

## Set How Concierge Sounds

On the same **Current Autopilot** page, the **Concierge voice** card controls how replies sound:

- **Tone**: Formal, Friendly or Casual.
- **Enthusiasm**: Reserved, Warm or Enthusiastic.
- **Says it is AI**: Never, Only if asked, or Always.
- **Hand-off message**: what Concierge tells a guest when it passes the conversation to your team, for example "Let me check with the team and get back to you shortly."
- **Let finished conversations end**: when the guest's question is answered, Concierge stops instead of sending a filler reply like "You're welcome!"
- **Written voice**: a description of how you write to guests. Click **Generate from my replies** to have SympleHost draft one from your past messages. Nothing changes until you click **Use this**.

![The Concierge voice card with Tone, Enthusiasm, Says it is AI, Hand-off message, Let finished conversations end and Written voice](/screenshots/autopilot-off-suggestion-on/concierge-voice.png)

💡 **Tip:** For anything more specific, like "never use emojis" or "sign off as The Ocean Stays Team", add a rule. See [Set Up Your Concierge](/platform-guides/organise-concierge-knowledge/).

## Pause Everything at Once

**Pause all automated guest messages** (at the top of the Current Autopilot page) stops every automatic message to guests: scheduled messages, Concierge auto-replies, booking emails and balance collection.

- Nothing is cancelled. Queued messages are kept.
- Concierge keeps drafting replies for you to send yourself.
- You can still send messages yourself as normal.

When you switch it back off, messages that came due during the pause wait in **Scheduled messages → Outgoing messages**, so you can choose to send or dismiss each one.

Only the account owner, an admin or a manager can pause and resume.

---

## When to Switch From Suggestion to On

Switch to **On** when:

- You've been sending most drafts without editing them for a week or two
- Your listing documents, General document and rules are complete
- Sensitive topics like refunds, damage and complaints are set to **Avoid** or **Alert** in your rules

You can switch back to **Suggestion** at any time.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Concierge isn't drafting or replying at all

Check three things: the mode in **Current Autopilot** isn't **Off**, the conversation's own **Autopilot** isn't set to **Off**, and **Pause all automated guest messages** is switched off.

### Concierge replies on its own, but I only wanted drafts

That conversation may be set to **Auto-replying**. Click its **Autopilot** setting and switch it to **Suggesting**.

### A draft keeps giving the wrong answer

Fix the knowledge, not just the draft. Check the listing's document, your General document and your rules, then try the question in the **Test** tab.
