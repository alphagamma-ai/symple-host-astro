Your SympleHost subscription invoices and receipts are in **Settings**, under **Billing**.

## Step 1: Open Billing

Go to **Settings**. In the left menu, under **WORKSPACE**, click **Billing**.

The Billing page shows your current plan at the top, such as **Pro Plan**, along with **Manage Subscription**, **Change billing cycle**, and **Change plan**.

## Step 2: Open an Invoice from Payment History

Scroll down to the **Payment History** card. This lists your recent invoices and receipts. The card says **Click an invoice to see details**.

Each row shows the invoice number, date, amount, and a **paid** status, with an open (external-link) icon on the right.

Click the invoice, or click its open icon, to open that invoice.

![Settings page with Billing selected under WORKSPACE in the left menu, showing the Pro Plan card and the Payment History card with paid invoice rows and an open icon on the right](/screenshots/download-invoices-and-receipts/billing-payment-history.png)

## Step 3: Download the Invoice or Receipt

The invoice page opens. It is hosted by Stripe. When the invoice is paid, the page shows **Invoice paid**, the amount, **View invoice and payment details**, and the invoice number, payment date, and payment method.

Click **Download invoice** or **Download receipt**.

The invoice is the bill for the subscription charge. The receipt is your proof of payment.

![Stripe invoice page showing Invoice paid, the amount, View invoice and payment details, and the invoice number, payment date, and payment method, with Download invoice and Download receipt buttons](/screenshots/download-invoices-and-receipts/stripe-invoice-page.png)
