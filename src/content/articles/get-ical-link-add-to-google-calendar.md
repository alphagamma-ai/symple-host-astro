Each listing in SympleHost has an **iCal export** link. Google Calendar, Airbnb, or Booking.com can subscribe to this link to see the listing's blocked dates.

This guide shows how to get the link and add it to Google Calendar.

---

## Step 1: Open the Property

1. Click **Listings** in the sidebar.
2. On the **Properties** tab, click the property you want.

---

## Step 2: Open the Calendar Sync Tab

At the top of the property page, click the **Calendar sync** tab.

![Property page showing the Details, Price Settings, Pricing Calendar, Property Settings, Management, and Calendar sync tabs, with an arrow pointing to Calendar sync](/uploads/get-ical-link-add-to-google-calendar/01-calendar-sync-tab.png)

---

## Step 3: Generate the Link (If iCal Export Is Off)

Look at the **iCal export** row.

If it shows **Off**, click **Generate**. The row then shows **On**.

![Calendar sync tab with iCal export set to Off, the text "Generate a link so Airbnb, Booking.com or Google Calendar can subscribe to this listing's blocked dates.", and the Generate button](/uploads/get-ical-link-add-to-google-calendar/02-ical-export-generate.png)

If it already shows **On**, go to the next step.

---

## Step 4: Copy the Link

When **iCal export** is **On**, the row shows the link. Click **Copy** next to it.

![Calendar sync tab with iCal export set to On, the export link (blurred), and the Copy button, above the Import calendars section](/uploads/get-ical-link-add-to-google-calendar/03-ical-export-copy-link.png)

---

## Step 5: Add the Link to Google Calendar

1. On a computer, open Google Calendar.
2. Next to **Other calendars**, click **+**.
3. Choose **From URL**.
4. Paste the link you copied.
5. Click **Add calendar**.

The listing's calendar now appears under **Other calendars** in Google Calendar.

💡 **Good to know:** Google Calendar refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule. Changes in SympleHost may take a while to show in Google Calendar.

---

## Keep the Link Private

The link shares the listing's blocked dates. Only add it to calendars you trust.

---

## Optional: Import Calendars into SympleHost

The **Calendar sync** tab also has an **Import calendars** section. It lets you subscribe to external calendars, such as Airbnb, VRBO, or Booking.com, to block those dates on this listing automatically.

1. Copy the calendar link from the other platform.
2. In **Import calendars**, paste it into **Add a calendar link**.
3. Optionally, enter a **Label**.
4. Click **Check feed**.
5. Click **Add feed**.
