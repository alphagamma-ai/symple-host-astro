Connecting Airbnb takes about five minutes. These three things surprise most hosts afterwards. Two minutes now saves you a worried first hour.

---

## 1. Airbnb Goes Grey. Your Bookings Follow Within 30 Minutes.

**Your calendar and prices on Airbnb lock the moment you connect. That's normal.**

From now on you change them in SympleHost, and Airbnb follows.

| What | When it shows up |
|---|---|
| Prices and availability on Airbnb | Almost straight away |
| Your existing bookings in SympleHost | Within about 30 minutes |

Your bookings are not lost. They're still on Airbnb, and they're on their way.

⚠️ **Don't disconnect while you wait.** It stops the import and you have to start again. Still nothing after 30 minutes? Contact support.

---

## 2. Your Airbnb Price Becomes Your Price on Every Channel

**SympleHost copies your Airbnb price as your base price. Every channel you add later starts from it.**

If your Airbnb price is your normal price, you're done. But if you set Airbnb higher to cover Airbnb's fee, that higher price follows you:

| | Price a night |
|---|---|
| Your normal price | ₹4,350 |
| Your Airbnb price (with ~15% for the fee) | ₹5,000 |
| Your base price after connecting | **₹5,000** |
| What Booking.com gets when you connect it | **₹5,000** |

**To fix it, right after connecting Airbnb:**

1. Set your base price to your normal price (₹4,350). See [Pricing and Rate Rules](/platform-guides/set-up-pricing-and-rate-rules/).
2. Add a **15%** markup on Airbnb. Airbnb guests still see ₹5,000. See [Set Markups for All OTAs](/platform-guides/set-channel-markups/).

Do this **before** you connect Booking.com or any other channel.

---

## 3. Your Airbnb Settings Are Now in SympleHost

**Nothing you set on Airbnb is lost. You'll find it under Properties → your listing → Manage → Airbnb settings.**

That's where you now change:

- Cleaning and extra-guest fees, weekend price, taxes
- Weekly, monthly and early-bird discounts
- Minimum and maximum nights, advance notice
- Instant Book, cancellation policy, check-in times, house rules
- Title, description, photos and amenities

Payouts and your Airbnb account details stay on Airbnb.

---

## Also Good to Know

- **Turn off Smart Pricing before you connect.** Airbnb stops using it once SympleHost sends your prices.
- **Instant Book switches off while connecting.** SympleHost remembers your setting so you can turn it back on.
- **Your Airbnb service fee may change.** Airbnb may move you to its host-only fee. This happens with any connected software.
- **Mapping to a listing you already have works the other way round.** Airbnb takes that listing's prices and dates from SympleHost.
- **Airbnb-only discounts and promotions stay on Airbnb.** Set them up again on other channels if you want them there.

---

## Thinking of Disconnecting?

Check the three points above first. Most first-week disconnects happen because bookings hadn't arrived yet, or a price looked wrong on another channel. Both can be fixed while you stay connected. Contact support and we'll help.

If you still want to go ahead, see [How do I disconnect Airbnb?](/platform-guides/faqs-integrations-and-troubleshooting/#how-do-i-disconnect-airbnb)
