Three things surprise most hosts after connecting Airbnb. Here they are in pictures.

## 1. Airbnb Goes Grey. Your Bookings Follow.

![Airbnb's calendar turns read-only while SympleHost becomes where you edit prices and availability. Prices reach Airbnb almost straight away; existing bookings arrive within about 30 minutes. Don't disconnect while you wait.](/screenshots/before-you-connect-airbnb/01-the-moment-you-connect.png)

Still nothing after 30 minutes? Contact support.

## 2. Your Airbnb Price Becomes Your Price Everywhere

![If you do nothing, a ₹5,000 Airbnb price becomes your ₹5,000 base price and Booking.com gets ₹5,000. Fix it first: set your base to your real price of ₹4,350, add 15% on Airbnb so guests still see ₹5,000, and give Booking.com its own markup.](/screenshots/before-you-connect-airbnb/02-price-flow.png)

How: [set your base price](/platform-guides/set-up-pricing-and-rate-rules/), then [add channel markups](/platform-guides/set-channel-markups/).

## 3. Your Airbnb Settings Came With You

![Go to Properties, your listing, Manage, Airbnb settings to change prices and fees, discounts, availability, booking rules, listing content and promotions. Payouts and your Airbnb account details stay on Airbnb.](/screenshots/before-you-connect-airbnb/03-airbnb-settings.png)

## Also Good to Know

- **Turn off Smart Pricing** before you connect. Airbnb stops using it.
- **Instant Book** switches off while connecting. Turn it back on after.
- **Airbnb may change your service fee** to its host-only fee. This happens with any connected software.
- **Airbnb-only discounts** stay on Airbnb. Set them up again on other channels.

Thinking of disconnecting? Talk to support first, most first-week problems are fixable while you stay connected. [How to disconnect Airbnb](/platform-guides/faqs-integrations-and-troubleshooting/#how-do-i-disconnect-airbnb).
