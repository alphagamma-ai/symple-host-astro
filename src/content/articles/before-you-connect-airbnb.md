Connecting Airbnb takes about five minutes. Most of the worries hosts have in the first hour come from three things nobody told them up front. Read these first and nothing will catch you by surprise.

**The short version:**

1. Airbnb's calendar and prices go grey the moment you connect. That's normal. Your bookings take a few minutes to show up in SympleHost.
2. Your Airbnb price becomes the starting price for every channel you add later. Fix it before you connect Booking.com or any other channel.
3. Your Airbnb settings don't disappear. They're in SympleHost under **Manage → Airbnb settings**.

---

## What Stays on Airbnb and What Moves

| Stays on Airbnb | Moves to SympleHost |
|---|---|
| Your listing, reviews, guests, payouts and existing bookings | Prices, availability, minimum stays and blocked dates |

Three more things Airbnb does when you connect any channel manager:

- **Smart Pricing stops.** Airbnb's Smart Pricing and price rules no longer apply, because Airbnb now takes prices from SympleHost. Turn Smart Pricing off before you connect.
- **Instant Book is switched off while connecting.** SympleHost keeps your setting so you can turn it back on afterwards.
- **Your service fee may change.** Airbnb may move you to its host-only service fee. This happens with any connected software, not just SympleHost.

💡 **Tip:** On the **Connect your Airbnb account** screen, click **What changes on Airbnb** to see this list again.

## 1. Airbnb Goes Grey, and Your Bookings Take a Few Minutes

When you connect, SympleHost takes over your Airbnb calendar, prices and availability. Airbnb shows those controls greyed out (you can see them, but can't change them there). That's how every channel manager (software that keeps your listings in sync across booking sites) works: one place in charge, so two systems never fight over the same calendar.

What catches people out is the timing:

- **Prices and availability** go to Airbnb almost straight away.
- **Your existing bookings** start importing about 4 minutes after your listing is mapped (linked to its SympleHost listing), and can take up to half an hour to all arrive if you have many.

So for a short while, Airbnb is greyed out and your SympleHost calendar still looks empty. Your bookings are not lost. They're still on Airbnb, and they're on their way to SympleHost.

💡 **Tip:** While you wait, open **Channel Manager** and watch the listing's status. When it changes from **Setting up** to **Live / In sync**, the first sync has finished. Then check your calendar.

⚠️ **Important:** Don't disconnect during this time to "get Airbnb back". Disconnecting part-way through stops the import, and you'll have to start the connection again. If your bookings still aren't showing after 30 minutes, contact support instead.

From now on, change your calendar, prices and availability in SympleHost, not in Airbnb. See [Airbnb and SympleHost Integration Overview](/getting-started/airbnb-integration-overview/) for what syncs and what stays on Airbnb.

## 2. Your Airbnb Price Becomes Your Price Everywhere

SympleHost keeps **one base price** for each listing and sends every channel that price, plus that channel's own markup (an extra percentage you add for that channel only).

When you import a listing from Airbnb, SympleHost uses **your Airbnb nightly price as your base price**, and copies the special prices, minimum stays and blocked dates on your Airbnb calendar too, so Airbnb guests see no change.

⚠️ **Important:** If you map an Airbnb listing to a listing you already have in SympleHost, it works the other way round: Airbnb takes the SympleHost listing's prices and dates.

That's fine if your Airbnb price is your normal price. But many hosts set Airbnb higher to cover Airbnb's fee. If you do, that higher price becomes your base, and the next channel you connect gets it too.

### A Worked Example

- Your normal price is **₹4,350** a night. You add about 15% on Airbnb to cover the fee, so Airbnb shows **₹5,000**.
- You connect Airbnb. Your base price is now **₹5,000**.
- Next week you connect Booking.com with no markup. Booking.com gets **₹5,000** on every night, and the prices you had set in the Booking.com extranet (Booking.com's own admin site) are replaced.

### How to Avoid It

Do this **right after connecting Airbnb, before you connect any other channel**:

1. Set your base price to your normal price, for example **₹4,350**. See [How to Set Up Pricing and Rate Rules](/platform-guides/set-up-pricing-and-rate-rules/).
2. Give the Airbnb listing a markup that covers the fee, for example **15%**. Airbnb guests still see about **₹5,000**. See [How to Set Markups for All OTAs](/platform-guides/set-channel-markups/).
3. When you connect the next channel, set its markup straight after mapping the listing, and check the price preview before you save.

💡 **Tip:** Discounts and promotions you set inside Airbnb (weekly, monthly, early-bird and so on) stay on Airbnb only. They don't carry over to Booking.com or other channels, so set those up again on each channel if you want them.

## 3. Your Airbnb Settings Came With You

Once Airbnb is greyed out, it's easy to think you've lost control of your fees, discounts and booking rules. You haven't. SympleHost copied them across when you connected, and you change them in SympleHost from now on.

To find them:

1. Go to **Properties** and open the listing.
2. Click **Manage**.
3. Click **Airbnb settings**.

Here you'll find what you used to set on Airbnb, including:

- **Prices and fees:** cleaning fee, extra guest fee, weekend price, taxes
- **Discounts:** weekly, monthly, early-bird and last-minute
- **Availability:** minimum and maximum nights, advance notice, how far ahead guests can book
- **Booking rules:** Instant Book, cancellation policy, check-in and check-out times, house rules
- **Listing content:** title, description, photos and amenities
- **Promotions and checkout tasks**

Change a setting here and SympleHost sends it to Airbnb for you.

Your payouts, payout methods and Airbnb account details are still managed on Airbnb.

## If You Want to Disconnect

Before you disconnect, check whether the problem is one of the three above. Most first-week disconnects happen because bookings hadn't arrived yet (point 1) or a price looked wrong on another channel (point 2). Both can be fixed without disconnecting.

If you still want to disconnect, see [How do I disconnect Airbnb?](/platform-guides/faqs-integrations-and-troubleshooting/#how-do-i-disconnect-airbnb) If you're not sure, contact support first. We can usually fix the problem while you stay connected.
