Connect your VRBO account to SympleHost from **Channel Manager**. You sign in with your VRBO username and password, verify with the code sent to your device, then pick which listings to link.

## Before You Start

Have the following ready:

- Your **VRBO username and password**.
- Access to the device where VRBO sends your verification code.
- The matching properties in SympleHost.

On the connect screen, channels that import bring your listings in as new properties; the others connect their rooms and rates to properties you already have. VRBO is shown as **Connects rooms & rates**, so set up the property in SympleHost first — for example, [import it from Airbnb](/getting-started/import-listings-from-ota/).

## Step 1: Choose VRBO

1. Go to **Listings** in the left menu and open **Channel Manager**.
2. Click **Connect OTA**.
3. On **Choose a channel to connect**, find **VRBO** under **Vacation rentals** ("Each listing is its own property — villas, apartments, homes.").
4. Click **VRBO**. Its tile shows **Connects rooms & rates** and **Login + verification code**.

![Choose a channel to connect screen with VRBO under Vacation rentals, tagged Connects rooms & rates and Login + verification code](/screenshots/connect-vrbo/01-choose-channel.png)

## Step 2: Sign In to Your VRBO Account

On **Connect your VRBO account**:

1. Enter your VRBO **Username**.
2. Enter your VRBO **Password**.
3. Under **Minimum stay applies to**, choose **Arrival date only** (selected by default) or **Every night of the stay**. VRBO needs this before your rates can go live. Most hosts use arrival date only.
4. Click **Sign in**. To pick a different channel, click **Back**.

![Connect your VRBO account screen with Username and Password fields, Minimum stay applies to options, and Back and Sign in buttons](/screenshots/connect-vrbo/02-connect-account.png)

### What the Minimum Stay Options Mean

- **Arrival date only** — the minimum stay is checked against the arrival date.
- **Every night of the stay** — the minimum stay applies to every night of the stay.

## Step 3: Enter Your Verification Code

After you sign in, VRBO sends a code to your device. Enter that code in SympleHost to verify your account.

## Step 4: Choose Listings

Continue to **Choose listings** and pick which VRBO listings to link.
