SympleHost connects to Airbnb through official OTA integration partners. This page explains how the connection works, what syncs, and what you still manage in Airbnb.

## Connect Airbnb

Connect Airbnb from **Channel Manager**: click **Connect OTA** and select **Airbnb**. Airbnb uses one-click sign-in, and you can import or map your Airbnb listings.

For the full steps, see [How to Import Airbnb and Connect OTA Channels](/getting-started/import-listings-from-ota/).

## Manage Calendar, Prices, and Availability in SympleHost

After you connect, SympleHost is where you manage your calendar, prices, and availability for Airbnb listings.

Editing these directly in Airbnb is not recommended. Make changes in SympleHost so they stay in sync.

## What Syncs with Airbnb

These sync with Airbnb:

- Reviews
- Guest messages
- Cancellation policy
- Listing descriptions
- Amenities

## Listing Settings for Airbnb

Each listing has its own settings for Airbnb in **Channel Manager**:

- **Listing mapping tab:** open the listing to see its **Booking window**, the **Re-sync** button, the **Syncing to Airbnb** switch, and the **Markup**, **Minimum nights**, and **Maximum nights** fields.
- **Channels tab:** click **Edit** on the Airbnb row, then click **Settings** on the mapped listing to see its **Price**, **Availability**, and **Booking** tabs.

See [How to Check a Listing's Settings for Each OTA](/platform-guides/check-listing-settings-per-ota/) and [How to Set Markups for All OTAs](/platform-guides/set-channel-markups/).

## Airbnb Discounts

Discounts and promotions you set inside Airbnb, such as the **Top-rated guests discount**, stay managed in Airbnb. SympleHost does not change or remove them.

See [How to Manage Airbnb-Specific Discounts](/platform-guides/manage-airbnb-specific-discounts/).

## Taxes

**Fees & taxes** in SympleHost applies to direct bookings only. OTAs manage their own fees and taxes, so set taxes for Airbnb bookings in Airbnb.

See [How to Set Up Fees and Taxes for Direct Bookings](/platform-guides/set-up-fees-and-taxes/).

## Security Deposits

Security deposits on Airbnb bookings are supported. Set them through the Airbnb settings panel.

## Automated Messages

Set up automated messages for Airbnb guests in **Messages** → **Concierge** → **Automation templates**.

If you also send automated messages from Airbnb or another tool, turn those off to avoid sending guests duplicate messages.

See [Set Up Automated Messages](/platform-guides/set-up-automated-messages/).

## Co-Hosts

If you co-host on Airbnb, replies you send from SympleHost appear in the Airbnb thread under the name of the Airbnb account that owns the listing. Airbnb decides the sender name, not SympleHost. Don't reconnect Airbnb under the co-host's account. Instead, sign your messages and add a sign-off to your automation templates.

See [Why Airbnb Shows the Listing Owner's Name on Replies Sent from SympleHost](/platform-guides/airbnb-replies-show-listing-owner-name/).

## Airbnb Host-Only Service Fee

Hosts who connect to Airbnb using software pay Airbnb's host-only service fee of 15.5%, deducted from the host payout. Airbnb is extending this fee to all hosts from **September 15, 2026** (outside the EU) and **October 13, 2026** (EU).

To offset the fee, adjust your prices or set a markup for Airbnb. See [How to Set Markups for All OTAs](/platform-guides/set-channel-markups/).

For details on the split fee and the host-only (single) fee, see Airbnb's [Airbnb service fees](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1857) help article.
