## What Checkout Pages Are

Checkout pages are direct booking pages for individual properties or services. They are useful when you do not need a full website link in every campaign, but you still want guests to book and pay directly.

Use checkout links for:

- Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and email campaigns.
- WhatsApp replies to guests asking for availability.
- QR codes in printed material.
- A booking button on an existing external website.
- A direct link to a service, tour, transfer, or add-on.

If you have a published SympleHost website, checkout links use that public website address where possible. If not, SympleHost can still provide a direct checkout route.

---

## Before You Share a Checkout Link

Check these first:

| Requirement | Why It Matters |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Listing or service is active** | Guests cannot book inactive, archived, or draft items. |
| **Direct booking is enabled** | The checkout page needs permission to accept direct bookings. |
| **Pricing is correct** | Guests see pricing from your listing or service setup. |
| **Availability is correct** | Calendar, service schedule, capacity, and restrictions affect what guests can select. |
| **Payment gateway is connected** | Online payment needs Stripe or your available payment gateway. |
| **Cancellation and deposit rules are reviewed** | Guests should see clear payment and cancellation expectations. |

## Open Checkout Pages

1. Go to **Websites**.
2. Open the **Checkout Pages** tab.
3. Filter by **All**, **Properties**, or **Services**.
4. Find the property or service you want to share.

Each row shows the item name, type, status, checkout URL, and whether direct booking is enabled.

---

## Enable or Disable Direct Booking

Use the direct booking toggle beside each property or service.

Turn direct booking on only when the item is ready for guests. If you are still fixing photos, pricing, availability, payment settings, or policies, keep it disabled until the booking flow is ready.

For properties, review the listing, calendar, pricing, fees, taxes, deposits, and payment gateway.

For services, review the service status, rates, booking model, capacity, availability, workforce or resources, and payment setup.

---

## Copy a Checkout Link

Use **Copy Link** to copy the checkout URL, then paste it into your campaign, email, WhatsApp message, or external website.

Use **Open** to test the checkout page before sharing it.

When testing, confirm:

1. The correct listing or service opens.
2. Dates, guests, or service options can be selected.
3. Pricing looks right.
4. Payment options look right.
5. Confirmation and guest details are clear.

---

## Create Short Links

Short links are useful for campaigns where a long checkout URL is hard to read or track.

1. Find the listing or service in **Checkout Pages**.
2. Enter a short alias if you want one.
3. Click **Generate Short Link**.
4. Copy the short link and use it in your campaign.

Examples:

- `villa-august-offer`
- `airport-transfer`
- `bali-retreat`
- `weekend-stay`

Keep aliases short and easy to recognise.
